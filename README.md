A broadsheet front page for Wednesday, 18 November 2026, drawn entirely in Python with no libraries: a cream paper sheet under a thin double navy-and-red frame, a masthead reading "THE WORLD IN ONE DAY" over a red date banner, and a lead panel reporting the US strike on Iran's Qeshm Island with its illustration of the missile site, inbound drones and intercept bursts over the Gulf; below it the second Ukrainian strike on the Kerch bridge with its collapsed span in the strait, and Xi and Putin at the APEC summit in Shenzhen under three flags. The bottom tier covers the doubling Ebola outbreak in DR Congo, the Russian military aircraft that went down in Siberia with 19 dead, and an In Brief column on Peltola's Alaska win, Herzog's mandate for Eisenkot, the four-nation demand on Gaza aid, a magnitude 6.0 quake in Papua New Guinea and the European market slide on Gulf tension.
